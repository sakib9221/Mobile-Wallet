# Mobile Wallet - Changelog (আপডেট লগ) 📱🚀

Here is a comprehensive summary of all major design improvements, optimizations, and visual balance corrections completed over yesterday and today. These updates are integrated dynamically in both **English** and **বাংলা (Bengali)**.

---

## 📅 June 9 - June 10, 2026

### 1. 🎨 App Title & Header Redesign (হেডার এবং লোগো ডিজাইন)
* **Stretched "Mobile Wallet" Header:**
  * Re-architected the primary title with an elegant, modern display layout.
  * Configured exactly **20.sp text size** paired with **2.5.sp letter spacing** to expand horizontally, creating a clean visual balance that fits perfectly in the background shell container and leaves no awkward spacing.
* **Unified Control Symmetries (বাটন সমান সমন্বয়):**
  * Replaced individual custom text-button alignments for "Settings" and "About Application" into identical icon-only circular pills (**40.dp x 40.dp** bounds).
  * Removed overlapping text descriptors ("Settings" and "About App") for a clean, futuristic glass-morphism aesthetic.
  * Preserved individual action-group gradient properties (Indigo-Purple for Settings / Emerald-Glow for About).

### 2. 📊 Fluid & High-Performance Transactions Ledgers (লেনদেন তালিকার মসৃণ ইউজার এক্সপেরিয়েন্স)
* **Category-Based Ambient Visual Glow (ক্যাটাগরি ভিত্তিক গ্লো ব্যাকগ্রাউন্ড):**
  * Configured active custom gradient card backings that reflect a dynamic translucent shade corresponding to their exact category colors (e.g. food = amber tint, salary = emerald tint, utilities = indigo glow).
  * Added subtle glowing outer card border gradients matched to categories to build high-end 3D depth.
* **Modern Directional Quick-Scan Indicators:**
  * Replaced simple visual dividers with premium vertical type pills (Teal-to-cyan vertical gradient representing Income, Vivid Red-to-pink gradient representing Expense).
* **Double-Framed Amount Receipt Badges:**
  * Framed total values snugly into a receipt-style badge with a dedicated boundary border and translucent fill padding.
* **Zero scroll-latency and Touch Scale Animations:**
  * Applied custom touch scale animations (`android16Clickable`) that optimize the view rendering to eliminate standard touch delays, resulting in a perfect 120Hz smooth list scroll.

### 3. 📐 Symmetrical Layout Alignments (সুষম লে-আউট ও অ্যালাইনমেন্ট)
* **Straight Balance Box Alignment:**
  * Perfectly calibrated the "Remaining Balance" (অবশিষ্ট ব্যালেন্স) main card’s boundaries to match the exact vertical and horizontal borders of the header elements and top panels.
  * Establishes a seamless, symmetrical grid from the topmost title down to individual transaction listings.
